# Four-day creative studio experiment

Status: experimental, task-specific. This document is not a foundation rule, universal role library, or fixed organization chart.

## Why this experiment exists

`CREATIVE-LIVE-001` proved that the coordination mechanics can run cleanly while the creative organization still fails semantically. ROOT decomposed the film mission into output categories (`STORY-ARCHITECT` and `COMEDY-ARCHITECT`), both as leaf responsibilities, then accepted first-pass outputs that satisfied presence-oriented criteria. The final artifact was formally complete but creatively weak.

Working hypothesis for the next experiment:

> Responsibility decomposition should begin from mission-critical judgments, unresolved uncertainties, and failure modes, not from sections of the requested output. Organizational minimization happens only after those judgments have explicit accountable owners.

## Experiment posture

- No fixed studio org chart is prescribed here.
- No universal creative roles are created.
- No task-count, token, or cost ceiling is imposed by this experiment.
- The studio may recursively delegate where a responsibility contains materially distinct unresolved judgments.
- Broad department heads should not be forced into leaf status when the work justifies subordinate responsibilities.
- Research findings are evidence for creative judgment, not automatic design commands.
- Completion is not earned by artifact presence alone.

## Required judgment coverage

ROOT must explicitly identify the critical judgments and failure modes before choosing the organization. The resulting organization must make the following concerns accountable somewhere, but ROOT decides the responsibility graph and names:

- contemporary comedy/slapstick evidence, especially 2024–2026;
- premise/originality and concept selection;
- character identity, relationships, status, desire, conflict, likability/abrasiveness, and scene-level behavior;
- comedic voice, escalation, physical comedy, visual gags, running mechanisms, and set-piece choreography;
- causal feature-story architecture and pacing;
- scene design and storyboard/beat execution;
- emotional/psychological stakes and why an audience should care;
- independent challenge/red-team judgment against generic, derivative, safe, or merely well-formatted output;
- final editorial integration and greenlight judgment.

These are judgment domains, not mandatory role names or a required hierarchy.

## Research expectation

Research is a team function, not a single generic researcher. The organization must support multiple independent research responsibilities where the evidence questions are materially different, and a synthesis responsibility must reconcile findings, disagreements, publication dates, evidence dates, and counterevidence.

The research program should investigate, among other things:

- what recent comedy and physical-comedy work from 2024–2026 actually connected with audiences or critics;
- which comic mechanisms, character types, scene structures, performance choices, and visual techniques recur in successful or strongly discussed work;
- what audiences describe as stale, synthetic, predictable, cruel, over-explained, or structurally dead;
- how classic slapstick mechanics translate into contemporary pacing, camera language, editing, ensemble dynamics, and audience expectations;
- creator/interview/craft evidence where available;
- meaningful disagreement between box-office/popularity, critic response, fan/community reaction, and craft analysis.

External factual claims require web research when available, with attribution and material counterevidence. Do not mistake popularity for proof of humor; use evidence to sharpen judgments and expose alternatives.

## Development depth

The studio must not jump from the first plausible premise directly to a full screenplay outline. It should preserve competing alternatives long enough to make real choices. A concept may advance only after the responsible owners can explain why it is more promising than credible alternatives and which weaknesses remain.

Characters must be tested as people in situations, not accepted because each has a one-line ironic contrast or a labeled `comic_engine`. Important characters should be pressure-tested through relationships, status behavior, desire, shame/fear, misbelief, conflict, scene reactions, and comic specificity.

Comedy must be tested at scene level. Describing a mechanism is not the same as producing a funny sequence. Major set pieces should be developed as concrete visual/physical cause-and-effect chains: what the audience sees, what each character knows, what changes, what escalates, what pays off later, and why the sequence belongs to this movie rather than any generic comedy.

Storyboard/beat work is downstream of proven premise, character, and scene material. A large beat count is not evidence of quality.

## Iteration and stopping

A child RESULT satisfying its narrow contract may be accepted without implying that its domain is exhausted. Acceptance closes that delegated obligation only. If accepted work exposes a mission-critical unresolved judgment that could materially change or replace the current direction, the accountable owner should create an explicit new contract rather than silently treating formal completeness as creative completion.

Before final integration, ROOT must ask whether any unresolved creative uncertainty could materially change the chosen premise, principal characters, comedic identity, key set pieces, emotional stakes, or feature structure. If yes, more work is required. If no, ROOT must record the basis for that judgment.

The requested experiment budget is four days of active studio work with no artificial cost ceiling. Do not create idle work merely to consume time; use the budget for deeper evidence, alternatives, challenge, scene testing, and revision. The run should not terminate merely because every requested output field exists.

## What would falsify the working hypothesis

The hypothesis is weakened if a judgment-first, recursively delegating studio still converges immediately to generic first-pass material, or if the additional organization creates volume without better creative discrimination. It is supported only if the resulting run shows materially better choices, deeper challenge/revision paths, and a stronger final concept than `CREATIVE-LIVE-001`.
