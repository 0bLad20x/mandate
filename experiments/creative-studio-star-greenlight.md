# Creative studio STAR greenlight amendment

Task-specific continuation amendment for `CREATIVE-STUDIO-LIVE-001` and successor studio runs.

This amendment is additive experiment state, not a foundation-governance rule. It preserves the prior run history and does not rewrite the earlier four-day experiment evidence.

## Why this amendment exists

The four-day framing was useful as an anti-premature-convergence pressure, but elapsed time is not evidence of creative quality. The observed run also reached a legitimate `PAUSE` because it had promoted representative audience laughter evidence into a greenlight blocker that the current autonomous environment could not itself obtain.

That is the wrong completion model for the intended studio experiment.

The studio should not stop because a clock elapsed, and it should not remain paused merely because one desirable external validation source is unavailable. It should continue autonomous research, development, challenge and revision until it has earned the strongest internally supportable production-candidate judgment, while clearly separating proxy evidence from real external audience evidence.

## Completion target

The success target is `STAR_GREENLIGHT`.

`STAR_GREENLIGHT` means: the accountable studio concludes that it has discovered an exceptional production candidate that is materially stronger than the credible alternatives it explored, has survived independent replacement-level challenge, and has no unresolved *internally actionable* blocker likely to replace its core creative direction.

This is intentionally stronger than `complete`, `coherent`, `well formatted`, or `good enough`.

It is **not** a factual claim that the film will become a commercial hit. Real audience response and market outcome remain external uncertainties until observed. The studio must not fabricate those facts.

## No fixed runtime

There is no required wall-clock duration, minimum number of Turns, minimum number of agents, token quota, or cost quota for `STAR_GREENLIGHT`.

Time is neither a pass condition nor a failure condition. Work continues while a material unresolved judgment, credible challenger, or actionable quality risk remains. Work stops successfully only when the accountable acceptance chain has evidence that further internally available work is unlikely to replace the core direction or materially improve the production candidate.

Do not create idle work merely to avoid stopping.

## Evidence hierarchy

Use the strongest evidence actually available and label its epistemic status.

1. **External empirical evidence**, when genuinely available: representative or at least real audience response, performed/animatic/rough-cut testing, market evidence, attributable creator/craft evidence.
2. **Independent proxy evidence** when external empirical testing is unavailable: cold-read or blind review by independent responsibilities that did not author the material, adversarial comparison, scene-only evaluation, swap/deletion/compression tests, alternative-premise challenge, and separately reasoned audience-perspective panels.
3. **Creator/internal judgment** as the weakest layer and never sufficient by itself for `STAR_GREENLIGHT`.

Proxy evidence must never be described as representative audience laughter data. Absence of representative audience evidence is a carried production-validation risk unless the seed explicitly makes real audience testing a required deliverable.

## STAR greenlight judgments

The exact organization remains ROOT's responsibility. Before successful completion, accountable owners must resolve or deliberately carry at least these judgments:

### SG-J1 — Premise dominance

The chosen premise must defeat credible materially distinct alternatives on comic generativity, specificity, feature sustain, character pressure and scene potential. A surviving alternative that could plausibly replace the winner after one more internally available test means convergence is not earned.

### SG-J2 — Character magnetism and separability

Principal characters must be behaviorally distinct under the same pressure, generate relationships worth following, and resist reduction to profession + ironic trait + function. Pair-swap, scene-removal, status-pressure and relationship tests should materially break when identities are exchanged.

### SG-J3 — Scene-level comic proof

Major comedy/slapstick sequences must survive independent cold-read/blind proxy evaluation focused on the dominant comic read, physical geography, information asymmetry, timing, escalation, reversal, commitment and payoff. A mechanically elegant scene that evaluators consistently experience as merely clever, technical or exposition-dependent remains a revision candidate.

### SG-J4 — Feature causality

Major comic choices and set pieces must change later options, relationships, knowledge, resources or rules. Deleting or permuting large sequences should damage the feature's causal architecture rather than leave a sketch collection intact.

### SG-J5 — Emotional and relational investment

At least part of the audience-investment case must survive removal of prize/career/competition outcomes. Relationships, betrayals, loyalties, shame, desire or conflict must matter because of accumulated behavior, not only because jobs or scores are at stake.

### SG-J6 — Distinctive comic identity

The film must have a recognizable comic voice and rule system that cannot be reduced without major loss to a generic trope chain. Research and independent challenge must identify what is specific to this film rather than merely competent genre construction.

### SG-J7 — Independent replacement challenge

At least one independent responsibility or board must be empowered to recommend replacement of premise, character, scene system or feature architecture. Review is not complete if it can only suggest polish.

### SG-J8 — Comparative editorial convergence

ROOT must record what the strongest remaining challenger is, why it loses, what evidence could still reverse the choice, and why no currently available internal test is likely to do so.

### SG-J9 — Production-candidate package

The final package must be coherent enough to hand to a real production-development process: title/logline, premise/world, character system, causal feature story, major set pieces, scene/storyboard development, tonal rationale, emotional stakes, research basis, rejected directions, challenge/revision history, unresolved external validation risks and integration traceability.

## Autonomous continuation rule

A nonterminal quiescent state is not automatically a reason to stop.

When no handoff remains, the accountable owner must ask:

- Is there a material unresolved judgment that can still be tested with tools/evidence available inside the studio? If yes, create/continue the appropriate contract.
- Is there a credible alternative that could still replace the leader? If yes, test it comparatively.
- Is the only remaining uncertainty genuinely external and non-actionable in the current environment? If yes, carry it explicitly as an external validation risk rather than fabricating evidence.
- Has the concept survived the available challenge set strongly enough to justify `STAR_GREENLIGHT`? If yes, return the final result upward for independent acceptance.

`PAUSE` is reserved for a genuinely non-substitutable external dependency required by the mission itself, not merely for desirable evidence that can be honestly represented as an unresolved post-studio risk.

## Current-run amendment

For `CREATIVE-STUDIO-LIVE-001` specifically:

- the previous 2-hour/4-day timing language remains historical evidence but no longer defines completion;
- RT-M4 remains a valid warning that actual laughter has not been empirically measured;
- lack of representative audience/performed evidence must **not by itself** block autonomous studio continuation;
- ROOT may create independent cold-read/blind proxy evaluation responsibilities for S1-S3 or other material, provided those results are explicitly labeled non-representative;
- after proxy challenge/revision, ROOT must re-evaluate P1 against held alternatives and may reopen premise selection if the leader does not dominate;
- RUN-AUTHORITY may accept SUCCESS only after ROOT returns a final result that satisfies the amended `STAR_GREENLIGHT` target and the still-applicable prior criteria.
